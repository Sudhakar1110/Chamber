How a matter moves through the chamber — from intake to closure.

Case Lifecycle

1
Intake
Client submits case via portal or desk. Creates the Legal Party and Legal Matter records.
Creates: Legal Party + Legal Matter
2
Registered
Matter created, first timeline entry added. Routing and limitation period set automatically.
Auto: routing, limitation
3
Active
Team reviews and activates the matter. Court, judge, and case type confirmed.
Sets: court, judge, type
4
Hearings & Documents
Track hearings and generate documents. Every event syncs to the matter timeline.
Draft → Review → Final
5
eCourts Sync
Status, orders, and cause lists sync from eCourts via hourly CNR polling.
Hourly CNR polling
6
Close
Matter disposed, archived, or withdrawn. Final timeline entry recorded.
